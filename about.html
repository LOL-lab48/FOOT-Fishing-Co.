About Us


FOOT Fishing & Co. started with a simple idea:

finding the right fishing gear shouldn’t be confusing or overpriced.


We’re a small team of anglers who wanted to build something better — a place where people can find rods and tackle that actually fit their needs.


Instead of just listing products, we focus on:

  • Helping beginners choose the right rod
  • Recommending gear based on real use
  • Keeping things simple and honest

We’re just getting started, and we’re building this alongside the community.

Every review, every order, and every bit of feedback helps shape what FOOT becomes.


Our Goal

To build a trusted fishing store that puts people before profit.
